Company Overview & Import Heritage

Setting the Gold Standard for Authentic Cosmetics in Bangladesh

Founded in Dhaka, Ruposhi International emerged from a singular realization: beauty consumers in Bangladesh deserved uncompromising access to 100% genuine international skincare, makeup, and personal care products—free from the risks of grey-market counterfeits.

As a licensed direct importer, we partner with authorized brand distributors in South Korea, France, the United States, Canada, and the UK. All products are air-freighted and stored in our temperature-regulated fulfillment center in Banani, Dhaka, preserving active ingredients like Vitamin C, Retinol, and Peptides.
